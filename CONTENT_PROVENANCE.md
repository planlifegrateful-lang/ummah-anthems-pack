# Content provenance and licensing

This repository is a distributable product pack. Before release, verify the rights and terms for every lyric, prompt, image, audio reference, brand element, and generated output.

## Distribution checklist

- Keep a release manifest for included assets.
- Include attribution and license terms where required.
- Do not include customer data, credentials, or private media.
- Confirm the commercial platform permits the intended use.
- Record relevant generation and source provenance without exposing secrets.

A repository license does not automatically grant rights to third-party or generated materials included in the pack. Validate those rights separately.
