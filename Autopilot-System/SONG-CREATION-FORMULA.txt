AUTOPILOT SONG CREATION FORMULA
Use this exact system to generate unlimited high-quality tracks in the same style.

STEP 1: CHOOSE THE CORE EMOTION + FAITH ANCHOR
Examples:
- Resilience → Tawakkul / “I counted on Allah”
- Identity → Five Pillars / Shahada
- Urgency → Ummah responsibility
- Growth through hardship → Pressure / Sabr
- Action → Start now / intention + effort

STEP 2: STRUCTURE TEMPLATE (always use this)
[Intro] – 2-4 lines, atmospheric or spoken
[Verse 1] – 8-12 lines, personal story + faith element
[Pre-Chorus] – 4 lines, rising tension
[Chorus] – 8 lines, memorable, repeatable, emotional peak
[Verse 2] – 8-12 lines, deeper or solution-focused
[Pre-Chorus]
[Chorus]
[Bridge] – 6-8 lines, shift in energy or direct call
[Final Chorus] – bigger version of chorus
[Outro] – short resolution

STEP 3: LANGUAGE RULES
- Keep it first-person or direct “you”
- Mix modern motivational language with authentic Islamic references (Allah, salah, sujood, ummah, sabr, tawakkul, deen)
- Avoid being overly poetic or archaic – make it feel like it could be said in a conversation or khutbah
- Rhyme scheme: flexible, prioritize flow and emotion over perfect rhyme
- Target 140 BPM energy even if tempo varies slightly

STEP 4: SUNO STYLE PROMPT FORMULA
“[BPM] [mood] motivational trap, cinematic [adjective] production, deep male vocals with [emotion], heavy 808s / atmospheric pads, [specific elements], polished commercial, radio-ready, [energy], no female vocals”

STEP 5: GENERATION PROTOCOL
1. Write full lyrics using the structure
2. Paste into Suno with the style prompt
3. Generate 2 versions
4. Pick the stronger emotional delivery
5. Create 30-45 second TikTok cut focusing on Chorus or Bridge + Chorus
6. Write 3 caption variations + 1 hard CTA

STEP 6: CONTENT MULTIPLIER
From one song you immediately get:
- Full track
- 3-5 short clips
- Lyric video potential
- Background for talking-head content
- Quote graphics
- Series potential (Part 1, Part 2 of the message)
